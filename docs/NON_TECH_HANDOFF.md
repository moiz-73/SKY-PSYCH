# SkyPsych AI - Handoff for a Non-Technical Presenter

Written for a person who will present this demo but is not an engineer.
**You do not need to type a single command.** Everything is a double-click.

---

## What this product does (in plain English)

SkyPsych AI watches for signs of tiredness in a person's eyes and head
movements and shows them as colored warning lights - a fatigue "check engine
light" for an operator. It runs entirely on the computer in front of you, saves
no video or photos, and only gives advice (it never takes control of anything).

## What is in the folder (the 7 double-click files)

1. `README_FIRST.txt` - the one-page "read me first" you should open today.
2. `00_SETUP.bat` - installs the demo programs; run once, takes a few minutes.
3. `01_VALIDATE.bat` - checks everything works; wait for **VALIDATION COMPLETE**.
4. `02_SIM_DEMO.bat` - the guaranteed demo; needs NO camera. Present this one.
5. `03_LIVE_CAMERA_DEMO.bat` - optional live version, only if you have a webcam.
6. `04_PITCH_PACKAGE.bat` - opens the talking points for your presentation.
7. `05_HELP.bat` - what to run if anything fails or you are unsure.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the **SkyPsych** folder on the desktop (or wherever it was saved).
2. Double-click **`README_FIRST.txt`** and read it - it is the plain-English
   version of everything below.
3. Double-click **`00_SETUP.bat`** once. Wait a few minutes while it installs.
   If a blue window asks a question, press any key. Do this only the first time.
4. Double-click **`01_VALIDATE.bat`** and wait until you see
   **VALIDATION COMPLETE**.
5. Double-click **`02_SIM_DEMO.bat`** and present. A simulated operator appears
   and gets progressively tired, then recovers. Press **Q** or **ESC** in the
   video window to close it when you are done.
6. Optional: double-click **`03_LIVE_CAMERA_DEMO.bat`** if you have a webcam and
   want to show the live version. If it fails, just use step 5.
7. If anything is stuck or confusing, double-click **`05_HELP.bat`**.

## What to say during the demo (5-6 short lines)

- **Green** means the operator is alert and normal.
- **Yellow** means fatigue is rising - worth a break soon.
- **Red** means fatigue is critical - action is needed now.
- **Microsleep** (a long eye closure) is the strongest alert the system gives.
- **No face** means the system honestly says it cannot see the operator -
  it never pretends everything is fine when it cannot tell.
- **Recovery** shows the reading returning to normal - a fatigue sign that went away.

## What it is NOT

- NOT certified aviation equipment.
- NOT a medical device.
- Advisory only - it does NOT prevent fatigue and must never be used for
  safety-critical decisions.

## Where the talking points are

- In the `docs/pitch` folder inside the SkyPsych folder (a one-page pitch plus
  talking-point notes).
- Just double-click **`04_PITCH_PACKAGE.bat`** and they open for you.
