# SkyPsych — Final Slide Notes

*(Speaker notes for the closing slide. The slide itself should carry the vision line and the three color chips; these notes are what you say.)*

## The slide should show
- **Vision line:** "Every tired operator should be able to see it coming."
- **Three color chips:** GREEN · YELLOW · RED
- **Privacy line:** "No cloud. No stored video. Advisory only."
- **Honest caveat line:** "Advisory research MVP — not certified aviation equipment, not a medical device."
- **The ask:** "Pilots. Feedback. Introductions."

## What to say

### 1. One-line vision
> "Every tired operator should be able to see it coming — before the incident, not after."

*(Pause. Let it land.)*

### 2. The three-state takeaway
> "Remember three colors. **Green** — alert and normal. **Yellow** — fatigue is rising, look now. **Red** — critical fatigue, act. That is the entire interface, and that is the entire promise: a reading a control room can act on at a glance."

### 3. Privacy
> "And because it runs on an ordinary camera, with no cloud and no stored video, it is a layer operators and unions can actually accept. It reads state — it does not store faces."

### 4. The honest caveat
> "In the interest of trust: this is an advisory research MVP today. It is not certified aviation equipment and it is not a medical device. It estimates fatigue — it does not prevent it, and it never replaces the human. We say that to you now, because we would rather earn your trust honestly than lose it later."

### 5. The ask
> "So the ask is simple. We want **pilots for demonstration**, **honest feedback**, and **introductions to aviation or defense program managers**. Give us twenty minutes with your operators, and we'll show you what fatigue looks like before it causes the incident. Thank you."

---

### Delivery notes
- End **on the thank-you**, then stop talking. Do not fill the silence.
- If someone asks about accuracy or certification, answer in one sentence: *"Early and unvalidated at research grade — that is exactly what we want to fix with your operators."* — then point to the color chips again.
- The last image people remember should be the three colors. Repeat them once at the very end if you want: **"Green. Yellow. Red."**
