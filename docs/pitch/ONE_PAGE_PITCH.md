# SkyPsych AI — One-Page Pitch

## Problem
Human error dominates aviation incidents. Fatigue degrades perception, judgment, and reaction time **before** procedural errors occur — and it is often invisible, to the operator and to those supervising them.

## Solution
SkyPsych AI turns an ordinary camera into a real-time cognitive telemetry sensor. One unmodified webcam reads subtle, involuntary cues of fatigue — eyelid openness, blink rate and duration, eye-closure fraction, and head-pose drift — and fuses them into a plain interface anyone can understand.

## Why Now
Computer vision, edge compute, and fatigue science have matured to the point where this is possible without wearables or invasive hardware. Face-landmark models now run in real time on a laptop, privacy-preservingly, with no cloud and no specialized equipment.

## Product
SkyPsych AI is a local-only, privacy-preserving HUD that estimates operator fatigue in real time and presents it as a clear aviation-style severity readout:
- **GREEN** — normal, alert baseline.
- **YELLOW** — rising fatigue, worth a look.
- **RED** — critical fatigue; time to act.

The interface is advisory. It reports an estimate. It does not take control, and it never claims to detect a person it cannot see.

## Differentiation
- **No headset.** Only a camera already in the room.
- **No wristband.** No wearable to charge, lose, or refuse.
- **No cloud dependency.** Everything runs locally.
- **No raw frame storage.** Only abstract metrics are kept; your image is never saved or uploaded.
- **Advisory, not replacement.** It informs a human decision; it does not replace the operator.

## Markets
- **UAV ground control stations** — remote pilots, long shifts.
- **Defense simulation** — training runs that last for hours.
- **Commercial aviation** — fatigue risk management programs.
- **Rail, mining, long-haul trucking** — operators behind long hours.
- **Remote surgery** — exacting, sustained attention.

## Advisory Note
SkyPsych AI is an **advisory research MVP** today — it estimates cognitive fatigue and is **not** certified aviation equipment, not a medical device, and it does **not** prevent fatigue. It only reports, in real time, a telemetry estimate that can support a human decision.
