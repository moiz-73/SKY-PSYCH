# SkyPsych AI — Two-Minute Pitch

*(~350–400 words. Aim for about 2 minutes at a calm speaking pace. Pause on the demo, let the screen do the talking.)*

## The hook
Every aviation professional knows the same uncomfortable truth: human error is the leading cause of aviation incidents. And behind much of that human error rests something quieter and harder to catch — fatigue. It degrades perception, judgment, and reaction time gradually, long before an obvious procedural error. By the time a mistake is visible, the fatigue was already there for minutes. Our premise: that build-up should be visible too.

## The problem
Today, monitoring operator state usually means wearables — headsets, wristbands, EEG caps. They are uncomfortable, expensive to deploy across a fleet, easy to refuse, and hard to keep charged and calibrated. For a remote UAV operator on a long shift, the barrier is simply too high to use at all.

## The solution
SkyPsych AI removes that barrier. We turned the camera you already have into a cognitive telemetry sensor. Computer vision reads involuntary micro-signals in the face — eyelid openness, blink rate, eye-closure duration, head-position drift — and fuses them, locally and in real time, into a 0-to-100 fatigue index on an aviation-style HUD.

## The demo
Let me show you what I mean. *(Start the simulation.)* The system walks through every state: **GREEN** — a normal operator. Fatigue signals rise and it moves to **YELLOW**, then **RED**, flagging critical fatigue. It detects a **microsleep** and levels its highest alert. Coverage is lost and the system honestly says it **cannot see** the operator — it never draws a false "all clear." Then it recovers. *(Close the demo.)* All of that ran on one laptop, with no camera feed ever saved.

## Privacy
There is no cloud. No raw frames are stored. Only abstract numbers — blinks, closures, head position — are kept. Your image stays on the machine, which is why operators and unions can trust it.

## Markets
The fit is clear: UAV ground control stations, defense simulation, commercial aviation fatigue-risk programs, and the long-haul operators of rail, mining, and trucking — anywhere sustained attention is safety-critical.

## The ask
We are looking for pilots for demonstration, honest feedback, and introductions to aviation or defense program managers. Give us ten minutes with your operators and we will show you what fatigue looks like before it causes the incident.

## Honestly
A caveat in plain terms: this is an advisory early research system today, not certified aviation equipment, and not a medical device. It reports an estimate. It does not prevent fatigue, and it never replaces the operator — it helps the right human act sooner.
