# SkyPsych — 30-Second Pitch

*(~90 words, three short paragraphs, natural spoken language. Read it slowly and pause between paragraphs.)*

Fatigue is the quiet cause behind many aviation incidents — it slows judgment and reaction long before the visible mistake. Nobody can see it coming.

SkyPsych turns an ordinary webcam into a cognitive sensor. It reads the involuntary signs of fatigue — eyelid openness, blinking, eye closures, head movement — and shows green, yellow, or red on an aviation-style display. No headset, no cloud. Everything runs locally and privately.

One honest note: this is an advisory early system, not certified aviation equipment. It reports a fatigue estimate in real time, so the right human can make the right call sooner.

---

## Speaking tips
- Keep the whole thing under 30 seconds.
- Pause after "Nobody can see it coming."
- Smile on "green, yellow, or red" — it is the most memorable moment.
- Never rush the caveat. Saying it slowly builds trust.
